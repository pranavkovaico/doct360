
# Article Creation

## Overview

Articles are the main content units in a Document360 knowledge base. They are used to explain product features, processes, and troubleshooting steps.

## Steps to Create an Article

1. Open the Documentation section.
2. Select an existing category.
3. Click Create and choose Article.
4. Enter the article title.
5. Add the required content.
6. Use headings, lists, images, tables, and links to organize the information.
7. Preview the article.
8. Click Publish when the content is ready.

## Editing an Article

1. Open the required article.
2. Click Edit.
3. Make the necessary changes.
4. Save your changes.
5. Publish the updated version if required.

## Best Practices

- Use clear and meaningful titles.
- Organize content with headings.
- Keep instructions simple.
- Include relevant screenshots where useful.
- Review content before publishing.

## Conclusion

Article creation helps teams build structured and accessible documentation.
